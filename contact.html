We offer a 14-day hassle-free return and exchange policy. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and with all original tags attached. Final sale and clearance items are exempt from returns. Simply contact our support team to initiate the process.

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within India. Express shipping options are available at checkout, delivering in 1-2 business days.

Currently, we ship across India, UAE, and select European locations. International delivery times range between 7-14 business days depending on customs clearance.

To maintain the print and fabric quality, we strongly recommend washing all hoodies, tees, and printed garments inside out in cold water. Tumble dry on low or air dry for best results. Do not iron directly on prints.
